Integration map / owners & permissions

One portal. Their existing systems stay.

Connect the tools around Mindbody. Adelante becomes the operational intelligence and enquiry layer, not a replacement for the website, accounts or scheduling software.

Recommended connection sequence

1
Lovable website

Add a Chatwoot website widget or existing form webhook and event tagging; keep booking links pointing to Mindbody. Needs website administrator access.

2
Google Business Profile & Maps

Tag links to the studio site and track inbound click journeys. Old native Google Business chat is not available. Optional WhatsApp contact link if eligible.

3
Instagram, WhatsApp & email

Use approved Meta professional-account channels and official WhatsApp Business Platform if authorised. Email can feed the shared inbox. Permissions and fees to verify.

4
Shared inbox · Chatwoot Community

One team queue with ownership: routine approved FAQs, Eudo for bodywork, Sarah for rentals/events and humans for sensitive issues.

5
Mindbody, read-only first

Use scheduled authorised reports/exports for early pilot; upgrade to API/webhooks after credentials, subscription and privacy approval. Never self-confirm a booking.

6
Make / Activepieces + Adelante owner portal

Prepare replies and renewal segments, update verified results and create a daily management digest. Secure EU hosting, access controls and backups.
