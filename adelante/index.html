Why Adelante

Technology in the background. People at the front.

Adelante started with something we saw again and again. Over fifteen years in commercial roles, more than twelve of them in global payments, working with travel, hospitality and ecommerce businesses across the UK and Europe, good businesses kept losing money in the gaps: calls missed during service, bookings never confirmed, fees nobody checked, owners doing admin at midnight.

The big players fixed this with teams of engineers. Independent businesses were left with the hassle. We bring the two worlds together: the old world of knowing your customers by name and a team that cares, and the new world of AI that answers, reminds, checks and reports in the background.

Less time on admin, lower costs, and more time for your people to spend with your customers. Because however good the technology gets, people still buy from people.

Questions owners ask

Straight answers.

Will this replace my staff?

No. It takes the repetitive work off them, so they spend more time with customers. Anything tricky still comes to a person.

Will my customers know they're talking to a machine?

Yes. Customers are told they're talking to an assistant, and they can ask for a person at any time. Replies follow your tone, and you approve how it speaks before anything goes live.

What does it cost?

It depends on the modules you choose and the size of your business. The online audit and the first call are free. The deep-dive audit is paid, and its cost is credited against the modules you take, so you get a clear price per module before you commit, and each module aims to pay for itself.

What about my customers' data?

Your data stays yours. We only use what a module needs to work, and we explain exactly what that is before you start.

Does it work with the systems I already use?

Usually, yes. We connect to your till, booking system, accounting and payment providers where they allow it. We confirm exactly what we can connect during your audit follow-up, before you commit to anything.

I'm not technical. Is that a problem?

No. We set it up, train your team and look after it. You see results, not settings.
