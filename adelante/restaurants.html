The problems in this industry

Three leaks that happen during service.

Calls and DMs go unanswered while the room is full.

The team is serving. The phone rings, the DMs pile up, and the guest books somewhere that answered first.

Cost, worked example: 3 tables of 8 lost on one Saturday at €40 a head is €960. Over 30 busy weekends, about €29,000 a year.

Adelante: replies in the guest's language within five minutes, takes the booking, and holds the table.

No-shows leave the best tables empty.

No reminder, no deposit, no waitlist. The empty chair is found after service, when it can no longer be sold.

Cost, worked example: 4 no-shows a week at €45 is €180 a week, about €9,000 a year.

Adelante: sends reminders, asks for a deposit where you choose, and offers freed tables to the waitlist.

Stock, waste and reviews go unchecked.

Ordering from memory, waste found too late, and one-star reviews sitting unanswered until the next week.

Cost: not priced in the figures above. Waste and reputation are shown in your audit, using your own numbers.

Adelante: tracks usage against what you sell, flags waste, and drafts a reply for every review.
