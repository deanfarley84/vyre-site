The problems in this industry

Three leaks in the diary and the evenings.

No-shows, with no reminder and no deposit.

The client forgets, and the slot is found empty after the appointment time has passed.

Cost, worked example: 4 no-shows a week at €45 is €180 a week, about €9,000 a year.

Adelante: sends reminders, asks for a deposit where you choose, and offers cancelled slots again.

Calls missed while the hands are busy.

Everyone is mid-appointment, the phone rings out, and the caller books somewhere that picked up.

Cost, worked example: €8,000 to €20,000 a year in appointments protected, depending on your volume.

Adelante: answers calls and messages while the team is with clients, and books the slot.

The evenings go on the rota and the reviews.

Rotas rebuilt from a WhatsApp thread, and a one-star review left unanswered for a week.

Cost, worked example: 5 to 8 hours a week of owner time, before any cash is counted.

Adelante: plans rotas from bookings, swaps by message, and drafts a reply to every review.
