The business case

Illustrative scenario using assumed volumes, improvements and costs.

11.7 hdesk time released

100 enquiries a month. Cutting desk time from 12 to 5 minutes per enquiry frees 11.7 hours. Moving from 20 to 23 paid jobs at €100 each adds €300 in monthly sales.

Return and break-even

At an assumed 60% contribution margin, three extra jobs contribute €180. Against an illustrative €150 monthly service cost, the net gain is €30 and cash ROI is 20%. Three extra paid jobs cover the cost. This calculation excludes the value of released time and any one-off setup cost.

How to measure the actual result
