The business case

Illustrative scenario using assumed volumes, improvements and costs.

135 hreleased per quarter
50%less handling time
€13,350extra quarterly commission

300 enquiries a month at 18 minutes each take 90 hours. At 9 minutes each they take 45 hours. Over a quarter, a modelled 17.8% increase on €75,000 in collected commission would add €13,350.

Return and break-even

At an assumed 60% contribution margin, the extra commission contributes €8,010. With an illustrative quarterly cost of €2,700, the net gain is €5,310 and cash ROI is 197%. Break-even requires €4,500 in extra collected commission. This calculation excludes the value of released time and any one-off setup cost.

How to measure the actual result

Track enquiry cohorts through attended viewings and completed sales. Match attributable commission to receipts, allowing for the sales cycle.
