The Sanctum Space

From a first-time visitor’s question to a class booking.

The business

The Sanctum Space is a yoga and wellness studio in Aloha Pueblo, Marbella. It offers group classes, private sessions and workshops, with bookings through Mindbody.

From a first question to a filled mat

A guest asks if a friend can join a class and whether a pass covers both places. Reception needs the studio’s pass rules and current availability. A clear answer and the correct booking link can fill a spare mat without interrupting the teacher.

Modules in this installation

Booking Rescue
Brings class enquiries together, prepares a reply and directs the guest to the relevant Mindbody booking link. Reception checks whether the booking was completed.
Return & Renew
Helps reception prepare a second-visit invitation or a renewal reminder using the guest’s contact preferences.
Owner overview
Groups open enquiries, follow-ups and bookings awaiting verification so the studio owners can prioritise the day’s work.

The business case

Illustrative scenario using assumed volumes, improvements and costs.

7.5 hreception time released
50%less handling time
€240extra monthly sales

150 enquiries a month. Reducing reception time from 6 to 3 minutes frees 7.5 hours. Moving from 60 to 72 paid drop-ins at €20 adds €240 if those places would otherwise be empty.

Return and break-even

At an assumed 70% contribution margin, 12 extra visits contribute €168. Against an illustrative €150 monthly service cost, that leaves €18 and a 12% cash ROI. Eleven additional paid visits cover the cost. This calculation excludes the value of released time and any one-off setup cost.

How to measure the actual result

Match enquiries to attended, paid Mindbody bookings. Exclude refunds, existing pass visits and transfers from other classes.
