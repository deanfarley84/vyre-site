What a free business review looks like

One problem. The numbers behind it. A fix worth checking.

This example shows the sort of working we do in a free 30-minute AI and Business Efficiency Review. Your real review uses your figures and circumstances.

Example only. Not a client result.

The numbers below are made-up inputs chosen to explain the calculation, not evidence of actual savings or guaranteed revenue.

01 · The leak

Calls and WhatsApps that never get answered quickly

Imagine a business misses 10 genuine enquiries each week because no one can reply promptly. We would first check whether those messages were really missed opportunities, not spam or duplicate enquiries.

02 · The assumptions

Put a value on the problem

  • 10 missed enquiries each week
  • 52 weeks in a year
  • 30% might turn into a booking with a timely reply
  • €60 average booking value

These are assumptions to test with the owner, not proven performance figures.

03 · The estimate

What the missed bookings might be worth

€9,360 per year

10 × 52 × 30% × €60 = €9,360 potential annual booking value. This is gross booking value, not profit. It excludes implementation costs and assumes every week has the same volume. Actual recovery could be lower or zero.

04 · One possible fix

Reply sooner without making your team work longer

Review the current WhatsApp, call and website enquiry flow. If suitable, propose an enquiry assistant to acknowledge messages, answer approved routine questions and hand off booking requests to a person.

We would agree the customer experience, access permissions and how the results will be checked before anything connects.
