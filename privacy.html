Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026 · Vyre, United Kingdom

Who we are

Vyre ("we", "us") is a United Kingdom based business providing independent payment intelligence to merchants (VYRE OS) and payment providers (VYRE Embedded). We are the data controller for personal data collected through this website. Company registration details will be published here on incorporation. This policy is made under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, as amended by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025. Contact: hello@vyrehq.com.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your personal data. Ever.
  • We do not hold your data for longer than you permit us to for the purpose of running your diagnostic.
  • We do not store your data beyond that purpose, and we do not reuse it for any other purpose, unless you give us your express consent.

What we collect

  • Request details: your name (if provided), company name, work email address, contact number, role, and the volume, merchant count and cross-border figures you submit through our free audit or VYRE Embedded forms.
  • Correspondence: emails and messages you send us.
  • Usage data: on Adelante pages only, optional HeyCatch analytics after your opt-in: page views, traffic sources, selected contact-button clicks and successful enquiry counts, using a pseudonymous browser identifier. No contact-form contents are passed to HeyCatch.

Why we process it (lawful basis)

  • To prepare and deliver the audit or conversation you requested: performance of pre-contractual steps taken at your request (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To respond to enquiries and follow up on your request: our legitimate interest in operating and growing our business (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Where you have given it, consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you may withdraw at any time.

Sharing and transfers

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with service providers who process it on our behalf, under appropriate safeguards. Our website forms are relayed by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which transmits your submission to our inbox; our email and hosting providers also process data incidentally to providing those services. Where data is transferred outside the UK or EEA, we rely on adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses.

Optional Adelante website analytics

We use HeyCatch, Inc. (United States) on public pages under /adelante/ to understand visitor journeys and whether visitors request a free business review. Analytics is disabled until you actively allow it. You can decline without losing access and change your choice through the Analytics preferences link at the bottom of an Adelante page. We also respect browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals.

Where enabled, HeyCatch may process page and session events, traffic attribution, selected button interactions, browser/device information, pseudonymous identifiers and technical network data. Our integration disables automatic click/form capture and masks element text and attributes. We do not send contact names, email addresses, business names, telephone numbers, form answers, or payment data as analytics properties. The SDK is not installed on VYRE OS merchant portals.

HeyCatch acts as our processor. Its providers may process information outside the UK and EEA, including in the United States, subject to contractual data-transfer safeguards. See HeyCatch's privacy policy and Data Processing Addendum. Their DPA states that customer analytics data is normally deleted or anonymised within two months after the subscription ends, subject to applicable exceptions. You may request access or deletion by contacting us.

Visitors in Hong Kong and Singapore

If you contact us from Hong Kong or Singapore, we handle your personal data consistently with the principles of the Hong Kong Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (PDPO) and the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) respectively, in addition to the UK GDPR standards described above.

Retention

Contact and diagnostic data: we keep your data only for as long as needed for the purpose of running your diagnostic. Before any contract, diagnostic request data is retained solely to prepare and deliver the diagnostic you asked for, within the scope you have permitted. If you do not proceed, we delete it. During a contracted engagement, we retain data only as needed to run the diagnostics under that engagement, and delete it when the engagement ends. The only exception is where a longer period is required by law, for example tax and accounting records.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to our processing of your personal data, and to data portability. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time. You also have the right to complain to us directly at hello@vyrehq.com, and to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or your local supervisory authority in the EEA. To exercise any right, email us and we will respond within one month.

Cookies

The general VYRE pages aim to operate without non-essential cookies. Adelante's optional HeyCatch analytics uses browser storage for its pseudonymous identifier only after you opt in. We store your analytics choice locally so we can honour it across Adelante pages. If you decline, no HeyCatch SDK is loaded. Use the Analytics preferences link on any Adelante page to change your choice. Rejecting analytics does not affect enquiry forms or navigation.
