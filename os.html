You already know

The problem is rarely the headline cost.
It is what the headline leaves out.

You can read your MDR. You can see your scheme fees. You probably know your chargeback ratio.

What is harder to see is the revenue that never lands: declines that might have been recoverable, decline reasons classified inconsistently across providers, FX spread inside settlement, retries that were never built, routing decisions nobody has revisited since the first integration.

Each of those is a question with an answer in your own data. VYRE OS asks the questions, sizes the answers and shows you the evidence.

How it works

Diagnose the cause. Prescribe the fix. Verify the value.

Every finding carries the stage it has reached, so an estimated opportunity is never confused with a verified outcome.

Measured incremental value after implementation, against a frozen baseline, reported to the C-suite as verified.

What we analyse

Seven categories.
Each sized on your data.

VYRE OS works through each category of your estate and reports what the evidence supports. Category estimates can overlap, so they are reported separately and are never added together automatically.

01
Potentially recoverable declines
Soft declines that were not retried, token and credential lifecycle gaps, issuer behaviour by market.
02
Decline classification
Inconsistent decline classifications across providers that hide the real reason a payment failed.
03
Cross-border performance
Local acquiring options not used in key markets, BIN-level performance differences.
04
FX and hidden costs
Spread inside settlement, currency mismatches, charged pricing that differs from contracted pricing.
05
Routing and retry behaviour
Single-route dependence, no failover, retry rules absent or too aggressive.
06
Chargebacks
Representment coverage, dispute ratios and the operational cost around them.
07
Payment method coverage
Local methods absent in markets where they carry a large share of checkout.

Each category is sized from your own authorised data. Where an external benchmark is used, the source is cited in the report. VYRE does not publish generic leakage percentages, because the figures that matter are specific to your stack, markets and data.

Technical capabilities

Connected in days. Nothing installed in your flow.

VYRE OS is designed to be adopted without an engineering project. It reads what your estate already reports, in an instance that is yours alone, and never touches a card number.

No-code integration
Connect without a build
Authorised reporting exports from your existing providers, or a read-only reporting connection. No SDK in your checkout, no change to your providers, no engineering sprint to get started.
Isolated profiles
Your instance, your data
Each customer runs in its own isolated instance with its own commercial profile: providers, contracted pricing, markets and processing lanes. No shared pool, no cross-customer visibility.
Read-only by design
Analyse, never execute
VYRE OS cannot route, retry, refund or change a provider setting. It holds no credentials that could. Your team keeps every control.
No card details
PAN, CVV and tokens never ingested
Card numbers are rejected at the point of ingestion and are not stored. Analysis runs on BIN, outcomes, fees and settlement data only.
Continuous cadence
Scheduled agent review
Daily, weekly or monthly review runs across every provider and lane, with open findings re-tested on each run and new ones surfaced as they appear.

Hosting. VYRE-managed, isolated per customer, in an agreed region. The deployment model, hosting region and the controls that apply are confirmed in writing during the security review for each engagement.

Common questions

Straight answers before you submit.

What does the free VYRE OS audit include?

An initial, evidence-rated view of where opportunities may exist across seven analysis categories, what the evidence supports, and whether further investigation is worthwhile. Findings depend on the data supplied. We aim to return initial findings within 48 hours of receiving sufficient usable data. The initial audit does not establish verified losses on its own; verification follows implementation.

How does VYRE OS get paid?

Your initial audit is free. Any ongoing analysis, implementation support or verification service is agreed separately before paid work begins. On merchant engagements VYRE takes no fees, referral payments or revenue share from PSPs, acquirers or orchestrators.

Does VYRE OS change anything in my payment stack?

No. VYRE OS is strictly read-only. It does not route, retry or process payments and does not change provider configurations. Your team controls implementation, and VYRE measures the outcome.

Do I need to change my PSP or orchestrator?

No. VYRE OS analyses your existing stack. Orchestrators and PSPs are infrastructure that moves money; VYRE OS is the intelligence layer that shows how that stack is performing and what to change. You retain control over your providers.

Which merchants is VYRE OS for?

B2C cross-border e-commerce, subscription, travel and other cross-border businesses processing more than £8m a year in card volume. VYRE OS is provider-agnostic and works with whatever PSP, acquirer or orchestrator you already use.

What data does VYRE OS use?

Authorised operational and reporting data from your existing payment stack, such as transaction outcomes, decline codes, fees and settlement reports. VYRE OS does not ingest or store PAN, CVV or payment tokens.
