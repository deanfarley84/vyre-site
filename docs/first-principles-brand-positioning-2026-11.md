# VYRE: first-principles positioning and website copy standard
**Approved drafting baseline:** 19 November 2026. **Audience priority:** end customers first; investor clarity through business model and measurable outcomes. This is a messaging standard, not evidence of achieved customer results.

## Parent brand: VYRE
**What we do:** Find payment performance gaps, recommend evidence-backed action and measure whether changes delivered value.

**Shared method:** Diagnose the cause. Prescribe the fix. Verify the value.

**Two customers, two offers:** VYRE OS is merchant-commissioned independent advisory across the merchant's authorised payment estate. VYRE Embedded licenses a provider-scoped version of the same intelligence for payment businesses. The provider-funded assessment must disclose its scope to merchants.

## VYRE OS
**Who buys:** Merchants with meaningful payment volume, cross-border complexity and multiple providers or markets.
**Customer pain:** The business won the customer but may lose revenue to avoidable declines, misclassified failures, unexamined routing, fees and FX costs. Dashboards do not prove the value of a fix.
**Above-the-fold message:** *You won the customer. Don't lose the payment.*
**What we deliver:** Read-only diagnosis of authorised payment data; ranked practical recommendations; evidence and confidence levels; post-implementation measurement against a baseline.
**Outcome that matters:** Verified incremental net financial value after merchant-authorised changes, not speculative gross recovery.
**Commercial model to explain:** Merchant-directed paid diagnostic/ongoing assurance and potentially performance-linked contracts, always explicitly agreed.
**CTA:** Request a free initial audit.
**Never imply:** Automatic routing changes, guaranteed approval uplift, possession of card details, or that a hypothetical opportunity is recovered revenue.

## VYRE Embedded
**Who buys:** PSPs, acquirers, payment gateways, orchestrators and platforms.
**Customer pain:** Merchant relationships become commoditised when the provider cannot show the business impact of its services beyond processing metrics.
**Above-the-fold message:** *Don't just process payments. Prove the value you deliver.*
**What we deliver:** Licensed provider-facing intelligence that explains underperformance within authorised provider scope, generates recommendations and quantifies improvements attributable to implemented changes.
**Outcome that matters:** Measurable merchant value, with adoption, commercial retention and provider revenue as business goals, not promised results.
**Commercial model to explain:** Provider licensing and design partnerships, with specific scope and terms agreed commercially.
**CTA:** Discuss an Embedded working session.
**Never imply:** Full-stack independent audits across competitors when provider data is limited; guaranteed retention; changing payment flows without authorisation.

## Adelante by VYRE
**Who buys:** Service-led SMEs and operational teams, initially on the Costa del Sol.
**Customer pain:** Customer enquiries are scattered across calls, WhatsApp, email, website, bookings and CRM; slow response and repetitive administration waste time and opportunities.
**Above-the-fold message:** *Miss fewer enquiries. Spend less time on admin. Win more business.*
**What we deliver:** Connect existing tools, tailor workflow automation and AI support to the customer, preserve human review for sensitive decisions, and manage continuous improvements.
**Outcome that matters:** Reduced response times and manual hours; completed bookings, lead conversion and incremental revenue where measurable.
**Commercial model to explain:** Agreed fixed implementation scope plus ongoing management where needed; third-party expenses and additional work are disclosed.
**CTA:** Free AI and Business Efficiency Review (30 minutes).
**Never imply:** Every integration is live before testing; AI replaces accountable humans; ROI examples are verified customer outcomes.

## Copy hierarchy on every website
1. **Pain:** A problem the paying buyer immediately recognises.
2. **Outcome:** Specific business improvement in accessible language.
3. **Mechanism:** Brief explanation of the solution, without leading with AI architecture.
4. **Trust:** What happens to data, who approves changes, what is included and excluded.
5. **Proof:** Baseline, success metrics, credible examples clearly marked as illustrative until independently verified.
6. **One primary next step:** Free audit for VYRE OS, working session for Embedded, free review for Adelante.

## For investors
VYRE OS is merchant-side payment performance assurance; VYRE Embedded is payment-provider infrastructure intelligence; Adelante is a modular, customer-specific AI-enabled business operations service. The common competence is *diagnosis → prioritised remediation → outcome verification*. The products differ in buyer, sales cycle, deployment scope and economic model, so they must not be represented as a single undifferentiated product. Do not present projected market size, customer savings or retention figures as facts without validated evidence.

## Publishing rules
- Start with what a user is losing or trying to achieve, not the number of agents, models or integrations.
- State what the user will receive, the limit of service, and how value is measured.
- Brand identity, colour tokens, customer examples, logos, functioning links and existing demo assets are unchanged unless separately approved.
- Maintain English and Spanish homepage positioning parity for Adelante.
- Avoid absolute promises. Distinguish projected, observed and independently verified outcomes.
