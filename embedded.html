Security and data

Designed for a procurement review. Confirmed in writing for each engagement.

VYRE Embedded is designed so that each customer's data is analysed in its own isolated instance, VYRE's control plane receives only aggregate metering records, never transaction data, and no merchant can see another's data.

The deployment model, hosting region and the isolation controls that apply are confirmed in writing during the security review for each engagement. Deployment options beyond VYRE-managed hosting are assessed case by case.

No PAN, CVV or payment tokens are ingested or stored. VYRE describes the controls it operates, not certifications it does not hold.

Operating model

What a security team will ask, answered precisely.

  • Read-only analysis. Nothing in the payment flow, no routing or execution.
  • No PAN, CVV or payment tokens ingested or stored.
  • Designed as one isolated instance per customer; controls confirmed per engagement.
  • Control plane receives aggregate metering only, never transaction data.
  • Human review and approval before findings are released to merchants.
  • Provider-level approve, pause and stop controls per merchant are part of the operating model.
  • Security and data-handling documentation available as part of a procurement review.
Common questions

Before the conversation.

Does VYRE Embedded change routing or touch the payment flow?

No. VYRE Embedded does not route, retry or execute changes automatically and never sits in the payment flow. It analyses the data in scope, tells the merchant what is underperforming, what to change and what the change may be worth, and then measures the outcome once the change has been made by the merchant or by you.

Does VYRE see card numbers?

No. VYRE Embedded does not ingest or store PAN, CVV or payment tokens.

Is an Embedded assessment independent?

VYRE's ownership and technology are independent and provider-agnostic. An Embedded assessment is funded by the provider and covers the provider's products, rails and available data, and that scope is disclosed to the merchant. It is not presented as an unrestricted independent audit of the merchant's whole estate; that is VYRE OS, which the merchant commissions directly.

How is merchant data kept separate?

VYRE Embedded is designed so that each customer's data is analysed in its own isolated instance and VYRE's control plane receives only aggregate metering, never transaction data. The deployment model, hosting region and the isolation controls that apply are confirmed in writing during the security review for each engagement.

How is this different from VYRE OS?

Same intelligence core, different client and scope. VYRE OS is commissioned and paid for by the merchant, covers the merchant's whole stack and takes no money from providers. VYRE Embedded is licensed to you, the provider, for use with your own merchants within your products and rails. The two are never mixed on the same engagement.
