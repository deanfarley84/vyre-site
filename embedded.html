Security and data

Built for a procurement review, not around one.

VYRE Embedded is designed so that Vyre never holds your merchants' transaction data centrally and no merchant can ever see another's.

Each customer runs in its own isolated instance, hosted in the EU first and deployable into your own cloud. Vyre's control plane receives only signed, aggregate metering records per verified period. Never a transaction.

No PAN is ingested. BIN and non-PAN fields only.

Operating model

What a security team will ask, answered.

  • Read-only. Nothing in the payment flow.
  • No PAN. BIN and non-PAN fields only.
  • One isolated instance per customer. Hard data separation.
  • EU hosting first. Customer-cloud deployment available.
  • Control plane sees signed aggregate metering only.
  • Provider retains approve, pause and stop controls per merchant.
Common questions

Before the briefing.

Does VYRE Embedded change routing or touch the payment flow?

No. It never reroutes or changes anything automatically. It validates data integrity, then tells the merchant what is not working, where volume should go instead, why, how to make the change and what the recovery is worth, and verifies the result once live.

Does Vyre see card numbers?

No. VYRE Embedded does not ingest PAN. It accepts BIN and other non-PAN fields only.

Can one merchant see another merchant's data?

No. Each customer runs in an isolated instance with hard data separation. Vyre's control plane receives only signed aggregate metering records, never transaction data.

How is this different from the merchant service, VYRE OS?

Same method, different buyer. VYRE OS is sold to the merchant and paid for by the merchant alone. VYRE Embedded is licensed to you, the provider, for use with your own merchants within your remit. The two are never mixed on the same engagement.
