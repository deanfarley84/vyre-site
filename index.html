How it works

Diagnose the cause. Prescribe the fix. Verify the value.

Built from the ground up as an AI-native business, VYRE combines proprietary payment intelligence with advanced reasoning and human oversight to turn fragmented payment data into clear, prioritised action.

01
Diagnose
Where payment performance breaks down, across your existing stack, from authorised operational and reporting data.
02
Prescribe
What the issue may be costing, which corrective actions deserve priority, and the evidence behind each one.
03
Your team implements
You decide what changes and when. VYRE never routes, retries or changes provider configuration.
04
Verify
Whether the implemented change delivered measurable incremental value, measured against a frozen baseline.
The evidence standard

Every finding is labelled by the stage it has reached, so an estimate is never mistaken for a result. Verification is the point: the question is not what could be recovered, but what was.

Stage 1
Estimated opportunity
A sized, evidence-rated hypothesis from the data supplied. Not a result.
Stage 2
Recommended action
A prioritised change, with the reasoning and the trade-offs stated.
Stage 3
Implemented change
Made by your team or your provider, with the date recorded.
Stage 4
Verified outcome
Measured incremental value after the change, with the evidence status shown.

Human oversight remains central: people review findings, approve action and retain control.

Trust and data

Precise about what we do with your data, and what we never do.

VYRE analyses the operational and reporting data you authorise. It never sits in the payment flow, never holds credentials that can move funds or change a provider's configuration, and never makes a change on your behalf.

Findings are reviewed by a VYRE operator before release. Where a finding rests on an assumption rather than an observation, the report says so, with the confidence level attached.

Deployment model, hosting region and the isolation controls that apply are confirmed in writing during the security review for each engagement. We describe the controls we operate, not certifications we do not hold.

What a security review will ask

Controls, stated precisely.

  • Read-only. VYRE does not route, retry or process payments, and does not change provider configurations.
  • No PAN, CVV or payment tokens are ingested or stored.
  • Only authorised operational and reporting data, within the scope you agree.
  • Human review and approval before findings are released; your team retains control of every change.
  • Assessment scope is stated for every engagement, including any limits of a provider-funded assessment.
  • Security and data-handling documentation is available as part of a procurement review.

Common questions

Straight answers first.

What is the difference between VYRE OS and VYRE Embedded?

VYRE OS is for merchants: independent, read-only analysis of your own payment performance across your existing stack, with prioritised advisory and outcome measurement. VYRE Embedded is for payment providers: the same proprietary intelligence core, licensed so a PSP, acquirer, gateway or platform can give its merchants evidence-backed advisory and verify the value delivered through its services. Both are built on one intelligence core.

I already have a dashboard. Why do I need Vyre?

A dashboard shows you what happened. Vyre works at the next two levels: it explains what is breaking and what it may be costing, prioritises what to change, and then measures whether the change delivered incremental value. The output is a decision with evidence attached, not another view of the data.

Is Vyre independent?

Vyre is independently owned and its technology is provider-agnostic: it is not tied to any PSP, acquirer or orchestrator and analyses whatever stack is already in place. Independence in practice means three things: provider-agnostic analysis, a transparent assessment scope stated for every engagement, and recommendations backed by evidence. On VYRE OS merchant engagements, Vyre takes no fees, referral payments or revenue share from providers. VYRE Embedded is licensed to a provider, and the scope of that assessment is disclosed to its merchants.

Does Vyre change anything in my payment flow?

No. VYRE OS is strictly read-only. It does not route, retry or process payments and does not change provider configurations. It analyses authorised operational and reporting data, recommends changes, and measures the outcome after your team implements them.

How does Vyre use AI?

Vyre is built from the ground up as an AI-native business. Advanced reasoning supports analysis and prioritised advisory. Vyre integrates NVIDIA-powered AI reasoning to support payment analysis and advisory, with human review and approval. People review findings, approve action and retain control of any operational change.
